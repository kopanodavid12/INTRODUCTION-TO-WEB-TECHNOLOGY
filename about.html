About Richfield Connect

Richfield Connect is an exclusive, foundational student social engagement platform built specifically to support the academic and professional growth of our student body. Our mission is to provide a secure, digital environment where students can connect across campuses, share academic interests, exchange study resources, and cultivate meaningful professional networks.

Community Guidelines

To ensure a productive and respectful learning environment, all users must adhere to the following community standards.

  1. Maintain professional and respectful communication in all posts and interacrions.
  2. Ensure all shared academic materials comply with institutional copyrights and integrity policies.
  3. Refrain from pisting unauthorized commercial advertisements or irrelevant spam content.
  4. Respect fellow student privacy and confidentiality at all times.
